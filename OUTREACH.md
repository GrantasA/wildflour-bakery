# Outreach without pitching

The idea: **build the site first, then send it.** You are not asking for a
meeting, not selling yourself, and not describing anything. You send a finished
thing and a price. They say yes or they ignore it.

Nothing here needs a phone call.

---

## Who to target

Only businesses where a website plainly makes them money, and where they
currently have nothing or something broken.

**Good targets**

- Has a Facebook page but **no website** — the single best signal
- Has a website that is broken on a phone, or clearly abandoned
- Sells a service with prices people compare: salons, barbers, garages,
  dentists, tattoo studios, driving schools, photographers, small gyms
- Small enough that the owner reads their own email

**Skip**

- Chains and franchises — decisions happen elsewhere
- Anyone with a recent, good site
- Restaurants that live entirely on Wolt/Bolt — they already have a funnel
- Anything where you cannot find the owner's actual email

## Finding them

- **Google Maps**: search "kirpykla Vilnius", "autoservisas Kaunas". The listings
  without a website link are your list. This is the highest-signal source.
- **rekvizitai.lt** for company details and contact addresses
- Facebook local business pages — the "About" tab usually has an email

Aim for **20–30 names**. Track them in `outreach/targets.csv`.

## Effort strategy

Building a demo takes ~25 minutes, so do not build 30 of them.

- **Top 5 targets** → build the real demo. Highest conversion by far.
- **Everyone else** → link the portfolio and the two existing demos instead.

Build more demos only once the first batch shows the emails get replies.

## The email

Short. No warm-up, no "hope this finds you well". Price stated upfront — it
prevents a negotiation conversation, which is exactly what you want to avoid.

**Subject:** `Padariau puslapį jūsų kavinei – pažiūrėkite`
(or `[Verslo pavadinimas] – svetainės pavyzdys`)

```
Laba diena,

Padariau „[Verslo pavadinimas]" vienpuslapę svetainę ir įkėliau ją čia:
[NUORODA]

Pamačiau, kad savo svetainės neturite, tad pasidariau pavyzdį – su jūsų
paslaugomis, darbo laiku ir kontaktais. Veikia telefone, turi mygtuką
paskambinti ir Google žemėlapį.

Jei patinka – 400 €, ir ji jūsų: prijungsiu prie jūsų domeno, atiduosiu
failus, jokių mėnesinių mokesčių.

Jei neaktualu – tiesiog ignoruokite šį laišką, daugiau nerašysiu.

Pagarbiai,
[Vardas Pavardė]
[telefonas] · [el. paštas]
```

**Why it works:** they see the finished thing before they read a word of
selling. There is no ask, no meeting, no decision to schedule. The opt-out
line lowers pressure and does double duty for compliance.

You are a native speaker — adjust the tone until it sounds like you. Stiff,
over-formal Lithuanian reads as a mass mailing.

## Follow-up (send once, ~5 days later, then stop)

```
Laba diena,

Trumpai priminsiu apie „[Verslo pavadinimas]" svetainės pavyzdį:
[NUORODA]

Nuorodą laikysiu aktyvią dar savaitę.
Jei neaktualu – atsiprašau už trukdymą, daugiau nerašysiu.

[Vardas]
```

**One follow-up. Never two.** Two is where you become a nuisance, and it will
not convert anyone who ignored the first.

## Replies — copy-paste

### Interested

```
Puiku! Tada taip:

1. Atsiųskite logotipą (jei turite) ir 4–6 nuotraukas – arba parinksiu
   tinkamas nemokamas.
2. Patikslinkite paslaugas, kainas ir darbo laiką.
3. Per 2 dienas atsiųsiu galutinę versiją peržiūrai.
4. Kai patvirtinsite – apmokėjimas ir perkeliu į jūsų domeną.

Sąskaitą išrašau kaip individualią veiklą vykdantis asmuo.
```

### "Kiek kainuoja?"

```
400 € vienkartinis mokestis – be mėnesinių mokesčių.

Įskaičiuota: puslapio dizainas, jūsų tekstai ir nuotraukos, darbo laikas,
kontaktų forma, žemėlapis, pritaikymas telefonui ir techninis SEO
(struktūrizuoti duomenys, kad Google matytų jūsų darbo laiką ir adresą).

Domenas (~12 €/m.) ir talpinimas – atskirai, bet talpinimą galiu sutvarkyti
nemokamai.
```

### "Ar galėsiu vėliau keisti?"

```
Taip. Tai vienas HTML failas – bet kuris programuotojas gali jį redaguoti,
jokių prenumeratų ar uždarų sistemų. Smulkius pakeitimus pirmą mėnesį
padarysiu nemokamai.
```

### "Turime žmogų / jau darome"

```
Supratau, tada netrukdau. Jei kada prireiktų – nuoroda liks aktyvi
dar savaitę. Sėkmės!
```

Do not argue. Move on.

### "Pagalvosim"

```
Žinoma. Nuoroda veiks dar savaitę – jei per tą laiką neatsakysite,
suprasiu, kad neaktualu, ir daugiau netrukdysiu.
```

A soft deadline that you actually honour. No chasing.

### Wants a call

```
Galiu ir paskambinti, bet paprastai viską susitariame raštu – taip lieka
aiškus įrašas, kas sutarta, ir niekas nepasimeta. Parašykite, ką norėtumėte
pakeisti, ir padarysiu.
```

If they insist and it's a real €400 sale, a five-minute call is survivable.
Write your three points down first. But most will accept email.

## Rules that keep this legal in the EU

Cold B2B email in the EU is governed by GDPR and ePrivacy. This is not
optional, and following it also happens to make the emails work better.

- **Only published business addresses.** `info@`, `salonas@`. Never scrape
  personal addresses, never guess `vardas.pavarde@`.
- **Only relevant offers.** A website offer to a business with no website is
  defensible legitimate interest. Blasting everyone is not.
- **Identify yourself** — real name, real phone, real email. No pretending to
  be a company you are not.
- **Honour opt-outs immediately and permanently.** If someone says stop, they
  go in the CSV as `do-not-contact` forever.
- **Keep volume low.** 5–10 per day, sent individually. Never BCC a batch.

Low volume also protects your email from spam filters, so this costs you
nothing.

## When they say yes

1. **Invoice** as individuali veikla (5% income tax up to €20,000 — confirm
   VSD/PSD with an accountant).
2. **Payment**: half up front, half on delivery. For a €400 job, full payment
   on delivery is also fine and avoids friction.
3. **Domain**: register in **their** name, not yours. You do not want to own
   a client's domain — it creates a hostage situation nobody enjoys.
4. **Hosting**: Netlify or Cloudflare Pages, free tier. Connect their domain.
5. **Hand over** the files and the config so they are never locked in.

Say this out loud in the email — "you own the files, no monthly fees" is the
strongest differentiator against agencies charging €30/month forever.

## Realistic numbers

Sending 25 emails with 5 real demos built:

| | Expected |
| --- | --- |
| Replies | 3–6 |
| Genuinely interested | 1–3 |
| Sales | **0–2** |
| Revenue | **€0–800** |

**Zero is a normal outcome for a first batch.** It does not mean the approach
failed — it usually means the targeting was wrong (too big, no email, already
had a site). Adjust who you pick, not the email.

Time cost: ~2 hours building 5 demos, ~1 hour sending. The demos are reusable
as portfolio pieces regardless of whether anyone buys.
