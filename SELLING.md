Everything needed to put the site generator on Fiverr. Copy-paste ready.

Designed around one constraint: **you never have to initiate contact, speak
live, or improvise.** Buyers come to you, and every message you need to send
is already written below.

---

## The strategy in one line

Do not sell "a website". Sell **a local-business one-pager that ships with
valid Google structured data, delivered in 48 hours.**

Generic web design gigs lose to $10 sellers. That race is unwinnable and not
worth entering. The wedge here is real and a buyer can verify it:

- Most cheap gigs deliver a template with **no structured data at all**. Ours
  emits correct `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD, so the business is eligible for
  Google rich results — hours, address and rating in search. That is a
  concrete, checkable claim, not a vague promise.
- 48-hour delivery is genuinely true, because a build takes ~25 minutes.
- The sites are hand-designed, not drag-and-drop builder output.

**Honest limitation:** Fiverr's Lithuanian-buyer market is small, so the
primary gig is in English for the global market. Lithuanian is offered as a
bonus rather than the core pitch — it wins a few buyers, but it cannot be the
whole strategy.

## FAQ (put these on the gig)

**Do I need to buy hosting?**
No. I can deploy it free on Netlify or GitHub Pages, or send you the files for
your own hosting. There are no monthly fees from me.

**Can I edit it later?**
Yes. It is a single HTML file - any developer can edit it. No locked builder,
no subscription.

**Do you need a call?**
No. Everything works over Fiverr messages, which keeps a written record of what
was agreed. Send your details and I will send the site.

**What if I don't have photos?**
I use free commercially-licensed stock photos that fit your business, or your
own photos if you have them.

**Will this get me to the top of Google?**
No - and be careful with anyone who promises that. What I do is make your site
technically correct so Google can read your hours, address and services. That
makes you eligible for rich results. Ranking depends on many other things.

**Multiple pages?**
This gig is one page. Message me for a quote on more.

## Message templates

Copy-paste these. Never improvise, never reply while stressed.

### Buyer asks a question before ordering

```
Hi, thanks for reaching out.

Yes, that works. To confirm what you would get: a one-page site with your
services, opening hours, contact form and map, working on phones, with schema
markup so Google can read your business details. Delivered in 48 hours.

If you send your business name, services and hours, I can start as soon as you
order.
```

### Buyer asks for a call

```
Thanks! I work in writing rather than calls - it keeps everything documented,
so there is a clear record of exactly what was agreed and nothing gets missed.

Send me your details here and I will have a first version to you within 48
hours. If anything is off, tell me and I will fix it.
```

Do not apologise. Stated as a working method, this reads as professional.

### Delivering the order

```
Your site is ready - preview here: [LINK]

Included:
- Mobile and desktop layouts
- Your services and prices
- Opening hours (today's row highlights automatically)
- Click-to-call, contact form and map
- LocalBusiness schema markup

Two things to check: that your phone number and opening hours are exactly
right. If anything needs changing, tell me and I will update it.
```

### Buyer wants something outside the package

```
Happy to do that. It is outside this package, so I will send a custom offer
for it - [PRICE]. Once you accept, I will add it to the delivery.
```

Never do free extra work to avoid an awkward conversation. The template exists
so you do not have to write it under pressure.

### Buyer is unhappy

```
Sorry that is not right - let me fix it. Can you tell me specifically which
parts to change? I will turn it around today.
```

Fix it fast and cheaply. Early reviews are worth more than any single order.

## Honest expectations

- **First order is the hard part.** With zero reviews, expect weeks of nothing.
  This is normal and not a sign the gig is bad.
- Fiverr favours new gigs briefly on launch. Be responsive that first week.
- **Response time is a ranking factor**, so enable app notifications and reply
  quickly — even just "Got it, I will come back to you today."
- Payments clear **14 days** after delivery for new sellers. Your first order
  is cash roughly three weeks after you publish, not immediately.
- Realistic first 90 days: **2–6 orders, €100–500 total.** Not a salary. It is
  real money, a review history, and portfolio pieces that make everything else
  easier.
