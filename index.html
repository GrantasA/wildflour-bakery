Our Story

About Wildflour

Wildflour started in a home kitchen in 2019 and grew into a neighborhood staple. Every loaf is made with slow-fermented sourdough starter, local flour, and zero shortcuts. We open at 7am and we're usually sold out by noon — that's how we like it.

Slow-Fermented

Our starter takes its time — every batch rises for hours, never rushed.

Local Flour

Milled by growers just outside town, sourced week to week.

Zero Shortcuts

Mixed, shaped, and baked by hand — the whole process, every time.
